Chosen excerpt (verbatim, ~115 words):
"Almost 70 years ago, in April 1956, this room was the setting of a major Cold War summit. Where Nikita Khrushchev came, after Stalin's death, with a delegation from the USSR. On the way here he stopped by Claridge's. Clearly nothing is too good for the workers. Before coming for dinner here, with Clement Attlee and Anthony Eden.
